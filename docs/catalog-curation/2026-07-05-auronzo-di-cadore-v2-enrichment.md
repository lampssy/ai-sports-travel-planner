- The reviewed Monte Agudo sources do not publish child-scoped snowmaking, glacier, snow-park, night-skiing, marked-freeride, or ski-day-apres facts.

- Official sources describe low-key post-ski activities but do not establish a dedicated local apres inventory for Auronzo.
