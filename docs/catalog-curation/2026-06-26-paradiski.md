## Caveats

- La Plagne's official online shop page was open for weekly-pass structure review but did not publish selectable prices at the time of curation; La Plagne uses representative shared-domain adult prices from the linked official Les Arcs Paradiski ticket page.
- Paradiski lift count and difficulty split remain unresolved because the reviewed official pages consistently supported the 425 km linked piste total but did not expose a stable aggregate lift count or piste-kilometer difficulty breakdown in the same scoped source set.
- Stay-base quality, supported skill levels, lodging price ranges, rental examples, and rental price-quality fields remain estimates pending a dedicated sampling policy.
