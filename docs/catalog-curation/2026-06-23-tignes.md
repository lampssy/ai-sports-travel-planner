## Caveats

- The 300 km and 74-lift Tignes - Val d'Isere facts describe linked-domain terrain and are not copied into local Tignes ski-area piste facts.
- Local-only Tignes piste kilometers, lift count, and blue/red/black kilometer splits remain unresolved in reviewed sources.
- Stay-base coordinates and exact nearest-lift meter distances remain unresolved; access buckets and atmosphere tags are source-reviewed but normalized.
- Lodging price ranges, stay-base quality tiers, supported skill levels, and rental equipment prices remain curated estimates pending a dedicated sampling policy.
- The linked Val d'Isere sector opens from 28 November 2026, while the modeled local Tignes ski area has progressive opening from 21 November 2026.
